**Beluga 2.2.0: Beluga on a server, and OpenCode as an agent.**

- **Beluga on a server**: put Beluga on an always-on box (a Linux server, a home PC, a Mac mini) with one install line and `beluga setup`. Then use it from your phone, or open the whole Beluga UI in any browser. `beluga web --tailscale`, `beluga connect user@host` (over SSH) and `beluga web --public` each give you a way in with one command. `beluga pair` pairs a phone from the terminal, `beluga project add` adds projects, and `beluga service install` keeps it running. In the app, Settings › Advanced › Web access lets a browser in with a one-time link or QR code, and removes it any time.
- **OpenCode as an agent**: OpenCode runs through Beluga like Claude does, asking Beluga before each step. Screens hide what an agent can't do.
- **What Waits on What on a busy board**: the graph fits the window, each epic's sub-tasks laid out in rows instead of one tall column. It opens at a zoom you can read, on the work in progress. It stays where you are while tasks change, and scrolling, zooming and dragging stay smooth. + and − zoom, 0 shows the whole board.
- **Tasks**: a worktree's agent decides whether its work is a task, and opens it as private and yours. Dragging a card opens the hidden columns (Backlog, Done, Canceled) so you can drop it there.
- **Plans**: a plan's pull request keeps up with its state, a finished plan keeps its row until it ships, and its PR shows above its conversation. A short milestone keeps its own height, and lines glide with their boxes when one folds or opens.
- **Your status gets an emoji**, shown next to your name in Friends.
- **Changes** shows a picture, GIF, PDF or video again once it's written, not the old one.
- **Projects**: adding a project back right after deleting it sticks, and the Add Projects sheet shows each folder as adding, added, or why not.
- **Search** no longer finds a deleted workspace's conversation (which then couldn't open).
- **Smaller things**: the panel button only shows where there's a panel to open, a long table's search box stays clear of its sticky headings, and Beluga's log stays small.

The phone app is 2.2.0 too, and it updates by itself. It offers every project on a server, even one with no conversations yet.
