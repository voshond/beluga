**Beluga 2.3.0: calls with your friends, and emoji reactions.**

- **Calls**: call a friend from a DM, a channel or a room's chat. The call lives in the conversation, as a strip under the header, with a pill in the sidebar while you're elsewhere and a floating card over other apps. Voice and cameras go Mac to Mac. Face to Face puts the call over the whole window, and Beluga has its own ringtone. Incoming calls reach the phone's Needs you.
- **Screen sharing**: share one window or a whole display. It's tuned for text (Sharp) or for motion (Smooth), behind •••. Everyone's pointer shows on the share. Click to ping, drag to sketch or mark a box, and Ask your agent puts the marked part, ink and all, in the chat. While you share and look at another app, a click-through overlay shows the others' ink, pointers and chat, and a soft frame reminds you that you're sharing.
- **Call notes**: during a call, each Mac writes down its own voice on its own machine, keeping no recording. If a Mac has no model or is slow, another Mac in the call writes its words down for it. Everyone gets one Call notes line in the chat, which opens a reader beside it with search, one person's words, and the call's timeline. Talk About It opens a private chat with your agent about the call, and agents look the notes up with tools when asked.
- **React to messages with emoji**, like Slack: hover a message for your most-used emojis or the full picker. It works in DMs, channels, rooms and workspace chats, on the Mac and the phone.
- **Friends you trust can use the board**: a friend's run gets its repo's board by your trust in them, and Full trust now counts outside a room too.
- **A friend's ask to your closed laptop** waits for it to wake up instead of failing after a day, says when it reached their Mac, and when it does fail, the asking Claude hears about it.
- **Your Mac's scratch folder** has its own Scratch tab, every Scratch tab shows how much the folder holds, and files nobody touched for 30 days go to the Trash.
- **Adding a plain folder** (one without git) offers Set Up Git instead of Try Again.
- **Uptime checks** in Sentinel no longer download the whole page.

The phone app is 2.3.0 too, and it updates by itself. You can react to messages there, and incoming calls show in Needs you.
