**Beluga 2.3.2: clearer calls and a call card that stays out of the way.**

- **Noise removal** is on in every call, so background noise stops reaching the others. Turn it off for one call from the microphone menu.
- **The call card** shrinks to the faces in the call and grows back when you point at it, with Stop, Mute, Leave and Hide. Whoever is talking slides to the front with a green ring, and the card turns soft rose while you're muted. Its buttons work on the first click, you can drag it anywhere, and it comes back to the same place after a restart. Hide works in any call.
- **Screen sharing**: the person sharing sees everyone's pointers and drawings again, also when sharing a single window. Viewers pick Sharp or Smooth from the ••• on the stage.
- **Fixes**: beside a shared screen, Send stays inside the composer. On a server, Ctrl+C the moment `beluga setup` asks Pair? now stops setup and closes the pairing, instead of quitting without a word.

The phone app hasn't changed: it stays 2.3.0.
