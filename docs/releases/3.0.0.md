**Beluga 3.0: your work on any computer you own, a sidebar that knows what needs you, and steering you can take back.**

- **Your computers**: pair a Mac mini, a cloud server or a Linux box in Settings › Your Computers, over SSH or with a one-time code, and your work runs there while the app stays on the Mac in front of you. Pick it per task with **Runs on** beside the mode and model, or send all new work there; Best Available picks the computer with the most room. One sidebar and one Needs You cover every computer. **Get Ready** clones a project a computer doesn't have and installs its tools after you say yes. Files, diffs and search feel local, a dev server there opens at the same `localhost` port here, and `.env` files only go after an ask that names them. Your settings follow you, Continue On and Move To carry work between computers, and one computer can be **Always On** for you on the network. Your phone reaches every computer directly, also with your Mac asleep. Each computer shows its own mark: its cloud, its distribution or its Mac model.
- **The sidebar sorts chats** into Inbox, Working, Later and Settled (turn it on in Settings). A merged PR settles its chat, and Remind Me Later quiets a chat until it's due. A **search field** at the top finds chats by title, branch or PR, then messages (⌥⌘F). Shift-click selects a range of chats to archive or delete at once.
- **Back and Forward** in the toolbar, with ⌘[ and ⌘], and the mouse's buttons. Each page comes back with the panels it had open.
- **Steering**: a steer goes in at Claude's next step and never stops the turn. A second steer joins the first. Cancel takes an unread steer back into the box, and a steered message reads as read the moment Claude takes it in. Settings picks whether sending during a turn queues or steers.
- **Usage limits**: a run stopped by your plan's limit continues on its own a minute after the limit resets. The card says when, with Continue Now and Don't Send.
- **Pull requests**: the toolbar names the real next step (Push N Commits, Resolve Conflicts, Checks running, Re-run Failed Checks). Merge pushes what the PR is missing first and never merges without it, and agents keep an open PR current. A merge shows as a line in the worktree's chat. A new **Pull Requests** page under Work lists every open PR.
- **Plans**: verifying a plan shows its four checks as they run, its latest line, and Watch It Work. Plans have no milestone limit, Plan with Tasks is back on a new worktree, and a plan waiting for review takes Send Changes.
- **Usage stats**: a roomy dashboard of your stats by project and agent, with the week's chart, your card and a recap.
- **Chats**: Claude's in-between words read quiet, and only its answer stands out. Regenerate Title writes a fresh title from what the chat is about now. Files and folders named in chat are links, also in backticks, and right-click shows them in Finder. A re-taken screenshot shows its new picture, and files you send your agent land in the Scratch tab's own Sent section.
- **Dictation**: pick which microphone it listens to.
- **Experimental** (new in Settings, off by default): **Live previews** draw a component your agent built right in its answer, with a control for each prop. **Sub-agent threads** let an agent start child threads and bring their results back together.
- **Fixes**: views that stall while loading offer Reload instead of staying blank; Delete no longer warns about a squash-merged branch's commits; the running step stays readable under Reduce motion; picking the login shell in Settings › Terminal sticks; on Windows, Open in File Explorer shows its window and Size on disk finishes measuring.

The phone app is 3.0.0 too, and it updates by itself. It shows every computer you paired and starts work there, sorts its Work tab like the sidebar, cancels a steer, says when a limited run continues, and shows a plan's checks as they run.
