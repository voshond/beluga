**Beluga 2.2.1: more themes, and a few fixes.**

- **Themes**: alongside Light and Dark, Beluga has its own Super Dark, OLED Black, Abyss and Arctic, plus well-known themes: Dracula, Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Nord, Gruvbox, Rosé Pine, One Dark, GitHub and Solarized. Pick one in Settings, where each shows a preview, or in the welcome's Make it yours. On OLED Black, cards and sheets get a fine light edge so they don't fuse with the black.
- **The conversation** fills the full window width by default.
- **What Waits on What**: task cards and epic boxes have their fill and edge again.
- **On a server**: the install line works in a UTF-8 terminal.

The phone app hasn't changed: it stays 2.2.0.
