**Beluga 2.3.1: better calls, forks, and finding a setting.**

- **Calls**: the caller hears a ringback until someone joins, and a call that would reach nobody doesn't start (the Call button says why). A ring nobody answers leaves "tried calling you" in the conversation and the inbox, with Call Back. Pick your microphone and speaker beside Mute.
- **Screen sharing**: Show My Pointer puts an arrow in your colour, with your picture and name, on the share. Sketches fade after a few seconds, there's a Box tool (or Shift-drag), and Ask sends the part you marked.
- **Call notes** keep one language for the whole call, start once someone else has joined, and are written by the best model in the call; the Notes button says which.
- **Settings has a search field**: type "dark mode" or "font size" and it takes you to the row. ⌘F comes back to it.
- **Forks**: pull requests open and merge on your fork, not the original. Sync Fork on the project home brings in the original, and Create PR on <original> sends a worktree's branch upstream.
- **Rename a worktree** from the sidebar, and its branch follows.
- **The Changes tab** can group its files by folder.
- **Beluga in a browser** has a gear in the sidebar with Settings and the menu bar.
- **Emoji reactions** stay in group chats; conversations with Claude no longer have them.
- **Fixes**: deleting or archiving a worktree stops the Claude working in it; /btw never leaves a blank card; a friend's task steps no longer look like they open; a picture in the chat is one card.

The phone app hasn't changed: it stays 2.3.0.
